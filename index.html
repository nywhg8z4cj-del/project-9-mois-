01 — About

Burgers, beer & the river at sunset

Barley on the Hudson is a relaxed waterfront spot in Tarrytown, built for long afternoons and easy evenings by the water. Grab a table outside, pour something cold and watch the boats drift past the bridge.

The kitchen keeps things simple and generous: burgers ground from premium Pat LaFrieda beef, tacos, lobster rolls, sandwiches and plenty to share, alongside a list of craft beers, wine and house sangria.

02 — How it works

No waiters. No fuss.

  1. I

    Order

    Line up at the outdoor kitchen counter and place your order.

  2. II

    Settle in

    Take your pager, find a table by the water and grab a drink at the bar.

  3. III

    Pick up

    When the pager buzzes, your food is ready at the counter. Enjoy.
